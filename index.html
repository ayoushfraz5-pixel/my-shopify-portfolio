About Me

Ayesha — Shopify Developer

I focus on building Shopify e-commerce experiences that hold together end to end — not just a homepage that looks good, but a store that's easy to browse, easy to shop on, and easy to manage afterward.

That means paying close attention to design and brand presentation, but also to the parts that are easy to overlook: how a layout behaves on a small screen, how quickly a product page loads, how clearly a collection is organized, and how the checkout flow actually feels to use.

Every store is different, so every build starts with the product, the audience and the goal — then the theme, sections and structure are shaped around that, rather than the other way around.
